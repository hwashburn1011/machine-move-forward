# Four enemy archetypes — reference breakdown

User reference: `reference/badmechs.png`. Each model is an original reconstruction; hidden sides and mechanical interiors are inferred.

1. **Bastion / heavy gunner**: largest mass, small recessed head, red almond eyes, oversized octagonal shoulders with 03 stencil, layered boxy chest, exposed rotary joints and pistons, thick forearm shield marked IRON / OBEDIENCE / LASTS, six-barrel rotary cannon, articulated thigh and shin shells, wide industrial feet.
2. **Revenant / blade assassin**: narrow dark chassis, tapered beak mask and red Y-shaped eye seams, angular sweeping pauldrons, stacked breastplate and open red-lit ribs, long blade-shaped limb armor, visible hinge axles, black scarf and torn waist cloth, two long edged swords, crouched asymmetric ready stance.
3. **Warden / rifleman**: dust camouflage, weathered helmet framing a black segmented mechanical face with one amber optic, scarf and broken-edged long coat, utility pouches and straps, metal-finger gloves, long scoped precision rifle with magazine, barrel collars and brake.
4. **Sovereign / commander**: tall narrow polished dark armor, horned crown, red eye slots, segmented amber halo behind the crown, brass-edged collar and ribbed breastplate, pointed hip armor, long front tabards with ORDER / BEYOND / HUMANITY stencil, burgundy-lined cape, raised claw hand, separate floating spherical red-eye drone.

Build and review sequence: silhouettes and proportion; armor layering and joints; facial construction; cloth and equipment; surface wear and typography; posed/front/back/detail renders; export checks and interactive review.
