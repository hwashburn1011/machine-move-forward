# S-07 gunner — reference build

Source: the user's `gunner.png`. This is a new standalone character asset; it
does not replace the currently published game's character.

## Features to preserve

- Broad, enclosed combat helmet with segmented shell, mounts, ear machinery,
  respirator filters, a deep blue wraparound visor and small cyan emitters.
- Heavy black/brown armor with extensive irregular tan wear, recessed panels,
  fasteners, retaining straps, rubber joints and overlapping shoulder protection.
- Dusty fabric with actual folds and a woven surface; a bulky scarf and a long,
  windblown, shredded shoulder drape.
- Separate belt pouches and larger thigh panels, including the visible slogans
  and S-07 markings. The geometric branch emblem is constructed as mesh detail.
- Backpack frame, exposed cables, antenna and long purple luminous canisters.
- Oversized industrial weapon with a separate receiver, sight, rails, cooling
  jacket, paired barrel assembly, cyan indicators and hanging brass ammunition.
- Restrained dark materials contrasted with cyan glass and purple canisters;
  detail should read in neutral lighting as well as the warm reference lighting.

## Deliverables

Editable Blender source, a neutral rigged character, separate weapon, posed
showcase export, Unreal-compatible FBX and portable GLB exports, packed PBR
textures, reference-angle and neutral front/back renders, and export validation.

The source only depicts one side and crops the feet and weapon tip. Back,
underside, boots and hidden attachment construction are designed consistently
with the visible forms rather than represented as recovered information.

Blender source axes: metres, Z up, character front −Y. The GLB exporter converts
to the standard glTF Y-up axes. Standard humanoid bone names are used for manual
retargeting; the rig is not advertised as an Unreal Mannequin skeleton.
