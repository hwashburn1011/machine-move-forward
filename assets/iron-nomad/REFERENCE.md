# Reference plan — four-legged mobile foundry

Source: user-supplied walker.png. Reconstruct the machine only; hidden sides require original design.

## Recognition priorities
1. Broad rectangular chassis on exactly four articulated legs; stance wider than the hull.
2. Three stacked deck/catwalk levels, warm open workshop cavities, dense exposed structural framing.
3. Two offset cylindrical furnace/exhaust towers, upper metal cages, orange hot vents and smoke anchors.
4. Front command tower and masts; blue navigation screen; large recessed turbine with a cyan arc.
5. Rust-red hanging banner with pale double chevrons; sagging rooftop awning.
6. Right-side lattice cargo crane with hook, four suspension cables and a hanging crate.
7. Floodlight cages, warm task lights, cool strips, ladders, stairs, antenna wires, hydraulic hoses and cargo.
8. Off-white weathered steel with sparse rust chips, dark recesses, rain streaks, worn brass handrails.

## Assembly
Meters, Blender Z up; front toward -Y. Main deck footprint 16m × 20m, lower/middle/upper deck surfaces near 10.6/14.2/17.8m. Mast tops around 31m. Four named leg chains: FrontLeft, FrontRight, RearLeft, RearRight. Moving subassemblies are rigid mechanical meshes beneath explicit pivots. These dimensions prioritize the reference silhouette; integration must adapt the game's existing smaller hull deliberately.

## Review
Render the complete silhouette, front turbine/command deck, banner/workshop side, legs and the rear. Compare each against the saved source crops. Preserve separate display-only smoke and stage lighting; exports contain exhaust/light anchors and geometry/materials. Deliver editable Blender master, full-detail GLB/FBX, a reduced-detail GLB, collider proxies, and a browser viewer.
