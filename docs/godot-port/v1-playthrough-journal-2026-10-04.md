## Evidence labels

- **Source inspection:** a hypothesis or implementation fact; not observed player experience.
- **Automated earned play:** normal clock/damage, live spawned supplies and reached production controls. Programmatic aiming/routes and minimum reading time are disclosed.
- **Native visual inspection:** actual rendered game images; does not establish human comprehension or performance.
- **Fixture/regression:** prepared states or targeted checks; not evidence of campaign affordability.

Every run will record source/asset identity, seed, saves, normal/assisted actions, elapsed time and its actual endpoint. A failed attempt remains in this journal. The final review will include successful moments as well as problems.
