## Short description

Build a home on a walking machine. Salvage the desert, repel hostile machines, and carry a fragile signal toward the last garden.

## Page description

The Iron Nomad keeps moving. You keep it running.

Machine Move Forward is a single-player survival adventure set aboard a three-deck walking machine. Reel in passing cargo, turn recovered supplies into useful equipment, and make the Nomad your own. Quiet stretches of maintenance and scavenging give way to boarding attacks, dangerous machinery and signals worth following.

Occasional robot watch towers overlook the route. Spot their silhouettes or incoming fire, take cover, and return fire from the deck. Their aim improves as the distance closes.

Step off the machine to investigate abandoned works and recover the parts, records and people that give the journey its direction. Restore the port recovery claw, earn utility drones, fit major systems to their service connections, and arrange ordinary equipment where it suits your home. Worn paint finishes and recovered keepsakes let the machine accumulate a history of its own.

This is a playable beta. Feedback on the first hour, story clarity, combat readability, building and performance will guide the next iteration. Start with **New Game** for the intended introduction. Separate **Playtest Checkpoints** let you inspect later features without replacing your campaign.

The opening begins with a handwritten human account that burns away into fragments of an industrial conflict before the rooftop escape. Press Escape to skip it and begin aboard the Nomad.

## Download and controls

Windows x86-64. Extract the entire ZIP and run `MachineMoveForward.exe`; keep its accompanying files together. No separate game engine installation is needed. The current renderer requires a Vulkan-capable graphics driver.

WASD moves, mouse looks, E interacts, F throws the salvage hook, B opens construction, Tab opens the wrist terminal, and Escape pauses. Left/right mouse fire and aim; R reloads. Controls, volume, music level and graphics quality can be changed in Settings.

Saves and settings live in your Windows user profile. The **Load Game** screen and Campaign Library have an **Open Save Folder** button. Playtest recording is optional, stays local and uploads nothing automatically.
