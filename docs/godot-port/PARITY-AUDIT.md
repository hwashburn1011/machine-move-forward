# Broader native parity audit — 23 September 2026

Compared the native game with the retained Three.js controllers and game integration, following the earlier grapple/radiation defect pass. This is a local native-port update; browser gameplay and source art are unchanged.

## Confirmed defects corrected

| Area | Native defect | Corrected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumables | Water and rations were consumed at full meters. | Full meters refuse use without spending an item; partial meters consume one and restore the original amount. |
| Production | Seed gardens stopped at three greens instead of six; incomplete output capacity could accept another full batch; oversized steps banked excess production. | Original six-green/two-water garden capacity, full-batch space requirement, preserved partial progress and bounded timers. Condensers stop without power; planters continue. |
| Machine research | Required a workbench, took an invented timed job and automatically fitted upgrades; lacked radio/safe-state gating and removal. | Research is a one-time instant purchase at a powered recovered receiver. Fit separately, one per branch; removal stays available with an unpowered receiver so power upgrades cannot trap the player. |
| Weapon attachments | Available before the Foundry, without powered tools; no removal; changing hardware retained pending reload/burst state. | Relay Foundry, working bench, safe onboard terminal and one tool power required. Paid unlocks persist, swapping/removal are free, and stale reload/burst state is cancelled. Temporary power is released outside wrist-terminal pages. |
| Damaged legs | Four wrecked legs reduced speed to 30% instead of the original 40%. | Matches the browser's continuous leg multiplier. The engine remains the subsystem that can halt movement; empty fuel retains emergency crawl. |
| Construction | A click could reuse approval from a previous piece, orientation or physics frame, including duplicate placement. | Recompute current aim, footprint, support, reach, unlocks, resources and attack state at the click. Cancelled/switched previews lose their stale targets. |
| Save recovery | Stored positions could embed the player in machinery or leave them unsupported; a stale piece counter could reuse a storage ID. | Validate placement after rebuilt collision bodies synchronize, recover to a clear supported deck, and advance IDs beyond existing pieces. |
| Save compatibility | Paid timed research and oversized producer timers needed migration; optional contacts could lack the record field used by their interactions. | Preserve already-paid research, clamp producer state to source limits, default missing contact records, reject invalid active upgrades/contact data before replacing live state. |
| L12 navigation | Stale path completion checks, retained velocity with no route, corner cutting and repeated floor contacts could stall/drift the companion. | Update navigation before consuming its state, stop without a route, follow tighter waypoints, step over bounded curbs and retry unspent movement only after floor-only contacts through another collision sweep. |
| Boarding mech navigation | Could stall along the same obstructed deck corridor and oscillate at its destination. | Tighter waypoints and shared floor-contact recovery; movement is capped to the remaining waypoint distance. Attack damage, range, speed and cooldown definitions are unchanged. |

Source checks included `Game.ts` consumable/research/fieldwork rules, `Producer.ts`, `SeedGarden.ts`, `UpgradeSystem.ts`, `MachineDamage.ts`, `BuildValidation.ts`, `PlayerCombat.ts`, `Weapon.ts` and their data definitions. Some suspicious behavior was deliberately retained after checking the source: upper-deck floor extensions need no neighboring floor, demolished supports do not cascade through other floor plates, and hitscan originates at the camera.

## Verification

- **132 new assertions** in `godot/tests/audit_parity.gd`, headless and on the RTX 3070 renderer.
- Production, garden, upgrade and leg-damage expectations are generated by executing the original TypeScript controllers with `tools/godot/audit-fixtures.mjs`, rather than copying native results into expected values.
- Actual authored turret collision tests cover powered automatic targeting, loss of power and mounted gunfire without self-hits.
- Navigation tests require L12 and a boarding mech to finish an eleven-metre deck route. L12 must stop at a newly inserted wall and stop when its navigation route is unavailable. The mech must settle at its destination without overshoot jitter.
- Save tests cover obstructed/missing supports, unique storage IDs, preservation of paid research, optional contact defaults and rejection of malformed data.
- Existing **108 integration**, **56 input/play parity** and **13 traversal/encounter** assertions rerun. These include all five accelerated chapter paths, scanner battle, ending checkpoint, optional stops, actual grapple inputs, death/recovery, stairs and gangway.
- Test saves stay in the isolated native-test directory. Personal campaigns and settings are not overwritten.

## Limits

This scan fixes the defects above and adds repeatable coverage; it does not certify every possible construction layout, route combination or long campaign session. Native physics, rendering and UI still have engine-specific differences. Browser-save migration remains unimplemented. The next acceptance step is an uninterrupted native playthrough with extensive building and save/reload use, alongside the original browser version.
