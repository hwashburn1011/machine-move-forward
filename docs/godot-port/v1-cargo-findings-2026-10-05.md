# Cargo usability findings — 5 October 2026

Evidence: source inspection, headless prepared fixtures and a coordinated native visual review. This audit follows the full-campaign journal's high scrap stock and repeated hook attempts. It does not claim a new uninterrupted campaign or uncoached player feedback.

| Finding | Player impact | Resolution / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual delivery already preserves partial cargo aboard; drone delivery already holds leftovers at its latch. Both keep opened contents through save/load. | A full store does not silently delete or reroll supplies. | Preserved existing transfer semantics. Focused actual manual and automated transfer fixtures verify exact accepted/remaining amounts, retries, save/restore and recovery after freeing room. Existing automation tests also verify held-load reload. |
| Targeted cargo showed READY based only on the hook trajectory, including a parked remainder with no compatible room. Throwing again just repeated an empty transfer and its notice. | Players could mistake capacity failure for a hook problem. | Existing bracket now distinguishes full/partial space and retained supplies; existing hook prompt identifies full retained cargo. Only an aligned, opened, parked remainder with zero transferable items refuses another throw. Freeing matching room immediately permits recovery. Sealed wilderness cargo, including first receiver recovery, remains collectible. No popup or alarm added. |
| Drone dispatch checked whether *any* scrap/components/fuel could fit, even when an opened crate contained only a different item. It also omitted the existing cosmetic salvage part from the possible sealed contents. | A known incompatible crate could trigger a futile pickup; a valid cosmetic-capacity case could be overlooked. | Dispatch now checks the actual known mixed contents against the destination dock. Sealed cargo is never sampled or rerolled to decide eligibility. Capacity simulation respects shared empty slots rather than counting one slot once for every item type. |
| The collector's actual interaction opens Storage, not Equipment, and did not explain its held load. | Clearing the personal pack instead of the dock could leave the player confused about why unloading stayed paused. | The collector Storage page now names held supplies and explicitly says to clear space in that dock's six slots. Power/combat/idle status remains quiet local text. Generic storage pages are unchanged. |
| Taking cargo from a dock while aboard pauses automation with the reader, so the held crate cannot unload until the reader closes. A damaged dock cannot resume at all. | Repeating a full-capacity message after space is freed, or promising unload at a destroyed dock, would mislead the player. | Status now distinguishes available room pending reader close; damage retains precedence while held contents remain readable. Focused destroyed-dock and native Take All/refresh checks pass. |
| One idle collector in the journal is not proof of a malfunction. | Range, path, power, cargo ownership and capacity can legitimately prevent dispatch. | No concurrency, range, flight-speed or idle behavior was changed without evidence. Existing tests continue to cover physical blockage, power loss, moving cargo interception and safe held-load recovery. |
