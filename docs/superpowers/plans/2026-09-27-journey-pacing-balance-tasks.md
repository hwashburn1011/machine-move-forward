# Journey pacing and resource balance

Status, 2026-09-28: **measurement tools and scheduling/recovery fixes implemented; observation-dependent balance changes awaiting evidence**. Lead owns shared gameplay integration; measurement and construction workers supply recorder/profile/resource evidence. Parent: [five-priority delivery plan](2026-09-27-five-priority-delivery-plan.md). S/M/L indicates relative scope.

## Baseline and limits

- `session.gd` advances the scanner through 180 active, eligible seconds, then a three-second pending handoff. Player/menu pauses, power loss, being off the Nomad and damage affect eligible time. UI/status/save validation currently also embed 180 and must change together if tuning changes the duration.
- `combat.gd::update_director()` already has a tutorial wait, calm/buildup/engagement/recovery, distance-based intervals, safe-health/menu/aboard checks, and sanctuary rules. `journey.gd` supplies protected radio intervals. Scripted route fights and optional patrols have their own triggers.
- `session.gd` burns fuel per active generator, modifies travel by weight/condition/upgrades and retains emergency crawl. Fuel items currently add one fuel point each. Crafting draws from its existing available containers and rolls back an output that cannot fit. Reload supply is unlimited; ammunition stock is not a new balancing lever for this pass.
- `route_chart.gd` and `native_progression.gd` already implement three-contact radar, optional equipment gates, recurring unrecovered discoveries and quiet-drive tradeoffs. The crane site already demonstrates heavy cargo.
- The current `journey_pacing.gd` is a 65-second forced-threshold fixture with invulnerability. It verifies a briefing grace interval, not a novice's campaign pacing.
- The 19 checkpoints intentionally supply generous materials and start at prepared stages. They are excellent functionality fixtures, but their resource abundance and skipped travel cannot establish campaign balance.

## Measurement and tuning policy

P01 supplies one optional, bounded local recorder used by all workstreams. No account, remote service, transmission, or background monitoring is part of this plan. Default normal play has recording disabled. Test commands/checkpoint review can opt in, and the report states this explicitly. This must not introduce extra game pause, alter the RNG sequence, or run a second campaign silently.

Proposed record contract: `{schema, run_id, source_hash, seed, mode, checkpoint_id, tuning_revision, wall_ms, simulation_s, phase, activity, event, payload}`. `mode` distinguishes synthetic/accelerated tests, prepared checkpoint play, and normal campaign play. A checkpoint restart begins a new segment; events never imply continuous campaign provenance. Include observer notes only in a separate report authored by the tester.

Use mutually exclusive primary dwell buckets: cinematic, menu, combat, expedition interaction/traversal, construction, salvage, travel, other. Keep secondary flags for overlapping activities instead of double-counting minutes. Track real elapsed time, unpaused simulation time and eligible scanner time separately. Coalesce repeated state; emit transitions/transactions and low-frequency summaries, using capped queues/files with explicit overflow counts. F02 adds test-owned frame probes to the same run identifiers.

Resource events record source, committed amount, recipient/result and before/after totals. Internal storage transfers are net-zero; failed operations earn/spend nothing; pending cargo and full-storage overflow remain pending until collected. Undo refunds are recorded only after authoritative success. Use event IDs to distinguish one transaction from repeated render/status messages.

Baseline first; tune one family at a time. Archive configuration and measured outcomes together. Do not promise a particular scan length, campaign duration, encounter frequency or performance gain before the baseline exists. Research/upgrade gates, robot-only survival and story order remain fixed unless a measured design issue is explicitly resolved within these tasks.

## Proposed experience targets to validate

These are review criteria, not claims about current player performance or promises to force every player's pace:

- No mandatory instruction leads to the wrong station or an unavailable route.
- Distinguish chosen downtime for building/exploration from involuntary idle waiting; record each prolonged wait and its cause before shortening a timer.
- A new unlock is explained where acquired and has a reachable opportunity to demonstrate its purpose within the next eligible travel/stop cycle, without a compulsory fight or extra chapter.
- Each tested mandatory milestone has a recoverable resource path after an ordinary mistake; unlimited refunds, abandoned storage and duplicate rewards are not valid solutions.
- The quiet-drive remains a readable speed/generation/detection tradeoff. Required funding and story completion do not assume it, the battery, the optional crane or repeated farming.
- The repeated test route preserves a stable combat/recovery rhythm under normal, low-fuel and heavily built machine conditions; exact interval targets are set after P02.

P09 and C05 can share the same recorded continuous human playthrough if their acceptance matrices are both covered. Synthetic tests remain separate and cannot mark the human criterion complete.
