# Emergency recovery and machine servicing: tasks

Date: 2026-09-28. Status: **ER01–ER08 implemented; ER09/ER10 have controlled technical evidence, with continuous-play and human balance acceptance pending**. Parent: [recovery and operations delivery plan](2026-09-28-recovery-and-operations-delivery-plan.md). This is priority 1. ER01–ER10 use S/M/L for scope, not elapsed time. The [player guide](../../godot-port/recovery-and-operations.md) describes the delivered behavior; the requirements below retain the full original acceptance scope.

Implemented details: the four authored chassis cabinets are the manual service ports. Normal refuel uses pooled whole fuel items with no fractional overflow debit; repairs retain existing prices. Foundry/Orchard return consoles provide separate local service pages and persistent pump stock. Assistance uses three 1.5-second holds with a release between steps; movement/reach/combat guards run in the world. Its initial aid is a 20-fuel tank floor, 50% engine floor for unaffordable critical damage, needed generator restoration, and an exclusive non-refundable fuel-burning loan source when missing. Request IDs/substeps/stock round-trip. Owned construction materials do not disqualify a missing-generator loan because a usable placement site is not guaranteed by inventory.

Controlled tests cover stranded fixtures, exact fuel/stock, stale or duplicate actions, cancelled/resolved requests, save restoration, stationary-safe service, actual cabinet access, large text and independent menus. ER09's complete post-recovery opening without prepared equipment and ER10's repeated-rescue economics remain pending; the implemented 4.5 seconds of holds is not a measured 30–60-second human recovery time. No universal combat recovery or balanced-campaign claim is made.

## Player experience and progression

Use the permanent chassis service panel from the first playable deck. Present the immediate fault, a normal solution with its exact cost, and an emergency option when no normal local restart is available. The first natural lesson is damage after the boarding defense; do not inflict tutorial damage or force service when the machine is healthy.

Place normal service bays at **Relay Foundry** and **Glass Orchard**, next to the common return path after required recoveries, so both route choices have equivalent access. They are optional stations with their own interface and finite persistent fuel stock. Array radar can subsequently point to existing fuel/repair contacts. Neither a service bay nor a radar signal is the last-resort recovery mechanism.

## Recovery ladder

| Situation | First action at the machine | Last resort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usable generators deliberately off | Restart locally or apply a valid travel plan. Explain the switch state. | No assistance award; a reversible control choice is not a supplies shortage. |
| Fuel in pack or stores, empty tank | Offer an exact amount to transfer into the shared tank, showing source bags and headroom. | No award while a normal refill can resolve the fault. |
| Damaged engine/leg/generator, affordable repair | Quote and perform the existing scrap-priced repair; show remaining operating costs. | No free substitute for a presently affordable local repair of the blocking fault. |
| Required load shed | Show what to disable/reprioritize or which existing generator to restart. | No repair reward for a resolvable configuration shortage. |
| Fuel exhausted or propulsion/generation broken, with no adequate local restart | Request assistance at the manual service port; complete a short on-deck service procedure when safe. | Assistance reaches the current Nomad, independent of its movement, receiver, scanner, inventory, cargo RNG or optional gear. |

Diagnose combinations together: repairing the engine while leaving the only generator destroyed is not a successful recovery. Pooled resource inspection is read-only; committing refuel/repair uses a single atomic transaction and the established bag order. Keep personal repair kits and comfortable-chair healing separate from machine service.

## Emergency assistance contract

The proposal is a small maintenance drone/service rig using a manual beacon at the fixed chassis port. It travels to the Nomad; the player does not travel to an automatically selected destination. Reuse the existing visual language of maintenance robots, but do not give L-12 an obligatory repair role or unlock it for free.

1. **Always accessible after the opening.** The port and its hand-powered request control do not require electricity or any item. It remains reachable if every player-built generator/floor is gone. A contextual marker can identify it; the wrist only records instructions.
2. **Explicit eligibility.** Diagnose a blocking fuel/propulsion/generation problem and try normal local fixes first. A low-fuel warning is advisory; a healthy, stocked machine cannot repeatedly claim assistance. User changes that restore normal operation before completion make the unnecessary parts of the award disappear.
3. **No campaign skip.** Requesting help leaves distance, lateral position, route, objectives, journals, scanner progress, cargo and outstanding encounters intact. It grants no story unique, research, construction materials, loose fuel item, component or companion.
4. **Combat remains real.** A request can be queued during combat, but assistance cannot erase enemies or repair through an active engagement. It deploys after existing threats clear. Once the safe, stationary service procedure begins, defer new ambient starts until it finishes or cancels; retain pending scripted encounters and their normal eligibility. Never reset an already active threat or its rewards.
5. **Short physical task.** Start with three simple actions at the same accessible port: isolate, connect, restart. Target roughly 30–60 seconds including arrival/presentation in a safe controlled case; this is a tuning target, not a forced countdown. Perform active holds in the world, with normal remapping/cancel behavior, rather than inside a paused aboard menu. Release, damage, death, movement or lost reach interrupts safely. Completed substeps may persist; partial button holds do not.
6. **Minimum service, not a full overhaul.** Initial candidates for testing: raise the shared tank only to 20 fuel when it lacks a viable refill; raise a blocking engine only to 50% health; restore a needed existing generator to working output; patch badly failed legs only as needed for useful movement. Never lower better fuel or health. Define the exact fault-dependent service floor in SR02 and verify the opening can run its 10-power refinery plus receiver afterward. Do not blindly refill or repair unrelated systems.
7. **Missing-generator case.** If no usable installed generator can be restored, attach one fixed loan starter unit at the service port. It provides at most one standard generator's output, uses the same per-generator fuel rate/modifiers, and cannot be stored, copied, sold, dismantled for materials or added to normal generator output. It withdraws when an ordinary repaired/installed generator takes over. Its enable switch remains reachable without power. Shared operations rules must handle it as a real fuel-consuming source, not synthetic capacity.
8. **Repeatable without a new dead end.** Do not use a once-per-campaign token, increasing mandatory price, consumable beacon, story-distance cooldown or debt that can disable assistance. A genuinely stranded player can recover again. Tank top-ups are direct, bounded and non-exportable; repeated use is a deliberate mercy mechanic with a time/condition tradeoff, not an assertion that supplies are conserved. Measure whether intentional repeated depletion becomes preferable to normal salvage/service and adjust the task and thresholds without removing the fallback.
9. **Durable and idempotent.** Persist request ID, phase, completed physical substeps and completion revision. Completion atomically applies only current deficits and marks that request fulfilled. Repeated callbacks, load during service, leaving a menu or reconnecting a visual drone cannot apply the same award twice. A new request requires a newly validated fault after the previous request ends.
10. **Bounded guarantee.** Acceptance proves recovery in a safe, living, accessible Nomad state, including zero fuel/stock/engine/generators and interrupted saves. It does not claim invulnerability or instant rescue during combat. Test requests queued through the tutorial, scout and carrier cases; no queue may consume or bypass those encounters.

The 20-fuel candidate is not a route budget. Engine condition, legs, mass and generator count change its useful distance. ER09/ER10 must demonstrate resumed salvage and required opening actions with this floor; increase or revise it if it creates a cycle of immediate breakdowns. Avoid speculative timer/reward tuning before those measurements.

## Ordinary service contract

- Existing scrap repair formulas remain the starting point, including full versus damaged subsystem cost. Show each requested repair and the total before one explicit service action. Do not charge for already healthy systems or debit partially on refusal.
- Add amount selection for refuelling from existing pooled fuel, capped by tank headroom. The proposed pooled transfer is a deliberate extension of the current pack-only `refuel()`; test bag provenance and full-tank fractions.
- Foundry and Orchard each start with a finite 20-unit pump reserve at a candidate price of **2 scrap per fuel unit**, bought directly into the tank. Both values are provisional, identical across route variants and recorded in the eventual balance report. Stock is per campaign site, never reset by reopening, reapproaching, loading or switching routes.
- The price/stock change is a new optional economy input: measure it alongside the existing 6-fuel optional cache and normal salvage before accepting it. Do not quietly replace current rewards or make the R-9 component exchange a mandatory refuel stop.
- Normal bays expose no free emergency bonus in addition to the same rescue transaction. A purchase rechecks cost, tank room, stock and local reach; payment and transfer are all-or-nothing. No stock withdrawal without delivery and no material refund exploit through fractional fuel amounts.
- Service access is captive, supported and independent of the Nomad's optional crane or player construction. Physical expedition actions, rewards, route records and departure checks remain unchanged. Assistance continues to work before bay access is earned.

## Specific review cases

- Starting before scanner repair with every inventory/store empty and no available cargo: assistance must not require `facts.salvage`, `fixed-radio`, a workbench, the cutter or L-12.
- Fuel in a distant aboard storage crate is a normal resource; fuel in uncollected world cargo is not counted as owned. A partial refill must show whether it actually resolves the current fault.
- A single generator at low health may leave the refinery dark. The assistance success condition is adequate essential function, not merely `capacity > 0` or health greater than zero.
- A healthy generator switched off is manually restartable even when the entire bus is dark. With multiple generators, assistance does not start and repair every optional load automatically.
- A destroyed/missing generator can be replaced by a loan source without paying build costs, creating a construction receipt or adding a refundable item. Ordinary newly built generators keep their normal costs and history rules.
- Loaded saves immediately before/after assistance completion and pump purchases yield the same counts as uninterrupted transactions. The current campaign save directory is never used for destructive fixture tests.
- Service can cancel without undoing paid repairs or resetting threats. During its active safe task, no new ambient spawn interrupts repeatedly; an already active encounter is not silently cancelled or rewarded.
- The drone/service rig is visual support, not a physics dependency. A visual load failure must leave the manual service procedure reachable and capable of completion.
- If the player cancels at a main or optional dock, the gangway and current destination remain intact. Assistance does not commit a new route or teleport the machine.
